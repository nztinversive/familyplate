Turn the food you already have into a practical plan for dinner.

FamilyPlate helps busy households track pantry ingredients, generate meal ideas, organize a weekly dinner plan, save recipes, and build a grocery list for what is missing.

PLAN FROM YOUR PANTRY
- Add ingredients manually or scan grocery barcodes
- Keep quantities, storage locations, and expiration dates organized
- See ingredients to use first

GET DINNER IDEAS
- Generate AI-assisted dinner suggestions based on pantry items
- Account for household preferences, allergies, dislikes, and available time
- Review ingredients and cooking instructions before saving or planning

ORGANIZE THE WEEK
- Build and adjust a weekly dinner plan
- Save favorite and custom recipes in your cookbook
- Add missing ingredients to a shared grocery list
- Mark meals cooked and record household feedback

SHOP AND COOK
- Scan groceries to add pantry items faster
- Share remaining grocery-list items
- Use step-by-step Cook Mode and adjust recipe servings
